## Data Security

Source: [Trust Center > Product Security > Data Security](https://security.twilio.com/?itemUid=38b62117-19f2-4e4e-b679-9421b66c114e&source=title)

Twilio supports TLS 1.2 to encrypt network traffic between the customer application and Twilio. Twilio is not able to guarantee that all connections to our carriers partners are encrypted, as we work with their connection options.

For media traffic, we offer the following security options:
- WebRTC: DTLS-SRTP w/ Signalling over TLS
- SIP: TLS-SRTP

Additionally, we offer customers the ability to push their media traffic over private connectivity or an IPSec VPN through [Twilio Interconnect](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007724794-Notice-Twilio-REST-API-s-TLS-and-Cipher-Suite-Security-Changes-for-June-2019). This option may not be available for all products.

Per NIST guidance, Employee & User Passwords must meet these criteria:
- At least 20 characters
- Three of four character types (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters)
- Cannot be any of your last 24 passwords
- Passwords rotation is no longer required
- Accounts lockout must occur after five invalid passwords attempts
- At least one day must have elapsed since you last changed your password
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required

Per NIST guidance, Privileged Passwords must meet the following criteria:
- At least 20 characters
- Three of four character types (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters)
- Cannot be any of your last 24 passwords
- Passwords rotation is no longer required
- Accounts lockout must occur after five invalid passwords attempts
- At least one day must have elapsed since you last changed your password
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required

## Service-Level Agreement

Source: [Trust Center > Product Security > Service-Level Agreement](https://security.twilio.com/?itemUid=54c702e6-3bd6-496c-a8db-72de85317206&source=title)

Twilio's [Service Level Agreement](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/service-level-agreement)

Twilio defines Service Level Agreements (SLAs) to provide clear, measurable commitments to service availability and performance. With a typical uptime guarantee of 99.95%, SLAs ensure reliability across Twilio's cloud-native platform. Unlike traditional Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs), which focus on recovery from downtime, SLAs emphasize continuous availability, leveraging Twilio's globally distributed, redundant infrastructure to minimize disruptions proactively.
