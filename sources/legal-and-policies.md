## Data Protection Addendum

Source: [Legal > Data Protection Addendum](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/data-protection-addendum)

The Data Protection Addendum is publicly available and governs processing of personal data by Twilio as a processor on behalf of customers.

Per section 12.2: Upon Customer's written request at reasonable intervals, and subject to reasonable confidentiality controls, Twilio will make available to Customer a copy of Twilio's most recent Audit Report. Customer agrees that any audit rights granted by Applicable Data Protection Law will be satisfied by these Audit Reports.

## Customer Audit Rights

Per section 12.2 of the Twilio Data Protection Addendum:

> Customer Audit. Upon Customer's written request at reasonable intervals, and subject to reasonable confidentiality controls, Twilio will make available to Customer a copy of Twilio's most recent Audit Report. Customer agrees that any audit rights granted by Applicable Data Protection Law will be satisfied by these Audit Reports.
